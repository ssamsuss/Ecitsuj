# Part II — Rules for Courts-Martial (Summary)

High-level reference summary of the procedural rules governing courts-martial:

- **Preliminary matters**: preferral, forwarding, and disposition of charges.
- **Pretrial**: pretrial confinement review, referral of charges to a court-martial.
- **Trial procedure**: arraignment, motions, presentation of evidence, findings, and sentencing.
- **Post-trial**: review and appeal procedures.

This is a general reference summary for simulation grounding, not the verbatim regulatory text.
