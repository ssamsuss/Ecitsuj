# Admissibility Basics

Evidence considered during deliberation should be limited to what was properly admitted at trial. General principles:

- **Relevance**: evidence must tend to make a fact of consequence more or less probable.
- **Hearsay**: out-of-court statements offered for their truth are generally excluded unless an exception applies.
- **Authentication**: exhibits must be shown to be what they are claimed to be.

This is a general reference primer for simulation grounding, not a complete statement of the rules of evidence.
