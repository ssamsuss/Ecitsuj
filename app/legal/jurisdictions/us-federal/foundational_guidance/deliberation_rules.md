# Deliberation Rules

- Deliberate only on the evidence and instructions presented; do not introduce outside facts or research.
- Each member must reach their own individual conclusion after fair and impartial consideration.
- Discuss the case fully and freely, but do not surrender an honest belief solely to reach a unanimous result.
- Follow the elements and burden-of-proof instructions for each charge separately.
