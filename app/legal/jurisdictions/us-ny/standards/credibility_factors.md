# Factors for Evaluating Witness Credibility

In deciding whether to believe a witness's testimony, consider all the circumstances, including:

- The witness's demeanor, manner of testifying, and apparent candor or bias.
- The witness's opportunity to observe the facts, and the accuracy of their memory.
- Any interest the witness may have in the outcome of the case, or any relationship to the parties.
- Prior inconsistent statements, and any explanation offered for a recantation or change in account.
- The extent to which the testimony is supported or contradicted by other credible evidence.

You may believe all, part, or none of any witness's testimony.
