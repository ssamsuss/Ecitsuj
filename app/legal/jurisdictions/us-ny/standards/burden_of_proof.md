# Burden of Proof: Reasonable Doubt

The People must prove the defendant guilty beyond a reasonable doubt. Proof beyond a reasonable doubt is proof that leaves you so firmly convinced of guilt that there is no reasonable doubt in your mind that the charge is true.

A reasonable doubt is an honest doubt of guilt for which a reason exists, based on the nature and quality of the evidence. If, after careful consideration of all the evidence, you have a reasonable doubt as to guilt, you must find the defendant not guilty.
