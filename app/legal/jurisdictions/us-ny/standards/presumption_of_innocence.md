# Presumption of Innocence

Under our law, a defendant is presumed to be innocent of the charge. This presumption is not a mere formality; it stays with the defendant throughout the trial and deliberation, and it can be overcome only if the People satisfy their burden of proving guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.

The defendant is not required to prove innocence or to present any evidence.
