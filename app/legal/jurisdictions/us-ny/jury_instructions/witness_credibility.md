---
As the sole judges of the facts, you must decide which witnesses to believe, and how much of a witness's testimony to accept. Consider each witness's demeanor, opportunity to observe, memory, possible bias, and consistency with other evidence.

You may accept part of a witness's testimony and reject other parts.
