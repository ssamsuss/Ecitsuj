---
Every person is presumed innocent until proven guilty. This presumption places upon the People the burden of proving guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.

Unless the evidence proves the defendant guilty beyond a reasonable doubt, the defendant is entitled to an acquittal, and you must find the defendant not guilty.
