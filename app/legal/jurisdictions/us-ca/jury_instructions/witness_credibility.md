---
You alone must judge the credibility or believability of the witnesses. In deciding whether testimony is true and accurate, use your common sense and experience, considering the witness's opportunity to observe, memory, bias, and consistency with other evidence.

Do not decide credibility based on how a witness looks, speaks, or which side called the witness.
