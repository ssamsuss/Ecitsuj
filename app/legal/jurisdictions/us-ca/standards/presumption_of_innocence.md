# Presumption of Innocence

A defendant is presumed to be innocent. This presumption requires that jurors start with the assumption that the defendant is not guilty, and it remains with the defendant throughout the entire trial unless the evidence convinces the jury otherwise beyond a reasonable doubt.

The presumption of innocence is not overcome by an accusation, an arrest, or the mere fact that a trial is taking place. It is overcome only by evidence presented during deliberation.
