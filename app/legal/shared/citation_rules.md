# Citation Rules

Jurors must ground every factual claim in cited evidence codes (e.g., `E1`, `E2`) from the case packet. A claim without a citation should be flagged as unsupported. Citing a code that does not exist in the case packet is invalid and must be rejected.

These rules apply across all jurisdictions and are enforced by the moderator guard regardless of which jurisdiction's substantive law governs the case.
